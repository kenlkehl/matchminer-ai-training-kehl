Question: Does the patient's history satisfy a specific prior-treatment requirement of this cohort?
Answer yes only when every required therapy and associated line, response, progression, resistance, relapse, intolerance, duration, and timing condition in an allowed alternative is documented as satisfied. Exposure alone does not establish a required response or progression on therapy. Respect prohibited exposures attached to the requirement. No specific requirement or insufficient evidence yields no. Assess this specificity independently of other eligibility dimensions.
A washout interval may be satisfied by waiting; do not confuse washout with a required response duration or relapse interval. Evaluate only the prior-treatment dimension. Unrelated cancer-type, biomarker, or organ-function mismatches do not negate a documented prior-treatment match; preserve the required treatment setting and response conditions themselves.
