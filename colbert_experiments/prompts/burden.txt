Question: Does a specific stage or disease-burden requirement of this cohort match the patient's cancer?
Answer yes only when the complete specified stage/burden requirement and any attached current disease-status condition are documented as satisfied. No specific requirement yields no (not_applicable). A contradictory match or unknown match yields no. Assess this specificity independently of other eligibility dimensions.
Evaluate ONLY stage, burden, and disease status. Do not turn an unrelated cancer-type, treatment, biomarker, age, or organ-function mismatch into a negative answer to this question. For example, metastatic disease satisfies a metastatic-stage requirement even if the patient's histology differs from the cohort's histology requirement. This single answer does not establish overall clinical fit.
